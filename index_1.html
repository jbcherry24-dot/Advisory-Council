About the Cohort

This isn't a program.
It's access.

The ELITE Madison cohort is a small, curated group of Director and VP-level technology leaders who are already operating at a high level — and are ready for the perspective, relationships, and executive exposure that most training simply can't provide.

Guided by three senior CIOs through boardroom dinners, focused sessions, and conference speaking opportunities, the cohort also connects participants with regional CIOs and academic professionals through virtual learning conversations. No slides. No spectators. Just the thinking that shapes how leaders lead at the highest level.

What participants gain

Executive-level decision making

A clearer sense of how leadership changes when the stakes are highest — how decisions get made, communicated, and owned at the C-suite level, not just managed.

Peer relationships that matter

A vetted, intimate cohort of peers navigating the same transitions — real relationships built over shared meals and honest conversation, not badge-scanning at a conference.

Sharper executive presence

How you show up in high-stakes rooms — and how that presence evolves as your scope and accountability expand well beyond your current role.

Judgment across complexity

Developing the ability to reason through business, technology, and organizational tradeoffs simultaneously — the kind of thinking that distinguishes executives from operators.

Conference speaking & visibility

Opportunities to speak and present at regional technology events — building a visible voice in the broader Wisconsin tech community alongside peers and practitioners.

Regional CIO & academic perspectives

Virtual sessions with regional CIOs and university professionals who bring research, cross-sector insight, and a longer view on where technology leadership is headed.

Advisory Council

Three CIOs.
One room.
No agenda except yours.

The Advisory Council isn't a panel or a keynote. Sandi, Sidd, and Ralph lead each session as peers — sharing how they actually think about the decisions, tradeoffs, and leadership challenges they face at the top. Curated by application and nomination, the cohort stays small so these conversations stay real.

Sandi Gardner

Chief Information Officer

Promega Corporation

LinkedIn Profile

Sandi Gardner is a technology leader with over 25 years of experience at the intersection of strategy, innovation, and people. As CIO at Promega — a global life sciences company headquartered in Madison — she has built a culture where curiosity and collaboration aren't aspirational values, they're how work actually gets done. Known for bridging the gap between technology and business outcomes, Sandi leads with a genuine commitment to developing the leaders around her: creating space for honest feedback, empowering teams to challenge assumptions, and ensuring that IT strategy stays tightly connected to what the organization is actually trying to accomplish. She brings that same commitment to the ELITE cohort — a belief that the best executive development happens in real conversations, not classrooms.

"She embraces change, is willing to challenge the status quo, and excels at clearly communicating her vision and empowering her staff to deliver on it."

Sidd Kuckreja

SVP, Divisional CIO

Lincoln Financial

LinkedIn Profile

Sidd Kuckreja has spent more than two decades building and transforming technology organizations across financial services and insurance — and he's done it at a level that gets noticed. Appointed SVP and Divisional CIO at Lincoln Financial in 2026, he previously served as CTO at TruStage (formerly CUNA Mutual), where he led one of the more comprehensive technology transformations in the Midwest — a journey McKinsey profiled as a model for high-ROI cloud adoption. His work spans enterprise architecture, platform modernization, digital transformation, and AI-enabled operations. He holds executive credentials from the Wharton School and Kellogg, serves on the Global Editorial Board for CDO Magazine, and advises the UW EBusiness Consortium. What makes him exceptional as an advisor: he's done it recently, at scale, and he's candid about what it actually takes.

“He has a rare ability to see across the entire enterprise — business, technology, and people — and mobilize all three toward the same outcome at the same time.”
Digital Transformation Cloud Strategy Financial Services AI & Data Enterprise Architecture

Council Member 03 / 03

Ralph Buelling

EVP & Chief Information Officer

UW Credit Union

LinkedIn Profile

Ralph Buelling brings 30 years of technology leadership experience across healthcare and financial services — and a rare ability to speak plainly about what executive leadership actually demands. As EVP and CIO at UW Credit Union, one of Wisconsin's top 10 financial institutions, he has built an IT organization that operates at the pace and complexity of a large enterprise while staying grounded in the credit union's core mission of member service. His career path — through Health Care Service Corporation, Dean Health Plan, and now UWCU — gives him a cross-industry lens that's genuinely hard to find. In 2024, he was named a Corporate Finalist for the Wisconsin CIO of the Year ORBIE Award, recognition from peers who understand what the role actually requires. In the ELITE cohort, Ralph brings that same directness: he talks about what's hard, not just what worked.

“Ralph doesn't just understand technology — he understands the organizational dynamics that determine whether technology investments succeed or quietly stall. That perspective is invaluable at the leadership level.”
Enterprise Architecture Cybersecurity Financial Services Healthcare IT Cloud Migration
